Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

This policy covers Hindu Verses Auto-Post ("the app"), a tool that uploads daily videos to the Hindu Verses YouTube channel and reports on how they perform.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the Hindu Verses channel signs in to the app. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access any other person's Google or YouTube account.

What data the app uses

The app asks Google for three permissions, all for the Hindu Verses channel only:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: to upload videos the channel owner has created, with their titles and descriptions.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly: to read the channel's own videos, their public view, like and comment counts, and the public comments left on them.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly: to read aggregate analytics for the channel's own videos, such as views and watch time.

The app uses this only so the channel owner can see which verses work well and reply to comments. Analytics are aggregate numbers and identify no viewer. The app does not read anyone's watch history, subscriptions, contacts, email or private data, and it never posts, edits or deletes comments.

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored as an encrypted secret in the channel owner's private GitHub repository and is used only by the scheduled jobs that upload videos and collect stats. Video stats and the text of public comments on the channel's videos, with the commenter's public display name, are saved in that same private repository so the owner can review them. Nothing is shared with or sold to anyone, and nothing is used for advertising.

Stored stats and comments are kept for as long as the channel is run, and are refreshed from YouTube at least every 30 days. To have a comment you wrote removed from these records, delete it on YouTube or email the address below, and it will be removed within 7 days.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page or the Google security settings page.

Viewers of the channel

Watching Hindu Verses videos on YouTube is governed by YouTube's own terms and privacy policy. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
